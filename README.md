# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 02, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application ("Game") created by Win Kyaw Myo. The Application is a game designed to allow users to play either in single-player mode or together with up to 8 players using a local offline WiFi connection.

### 1. Information We Collect
The Application does **not** collect, store, or share any personal information or user data. 
* **No Personal Data:** We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or photos.
* **No Location Data:** Although the game uses local WiFi to connect up to 8 players in multiplayer mode, it does **not** track or store your GPS or precise location data.
* **No Analytics or Tracking:** We do not use third-party analytics tools to track your behavior or usage within the app.

### 2. Permissions
The Application may request certain local network permissions solely for the purpose of enabling multiplayer connectivity over a local WiFi network for up to 8 players. This data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to any external servers.

### 3. Children's Privacy
Our Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal information from anyone, we knowingly do not collect personal identifiable information from children.

### 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. We will notify you of any changes to our Privacy Policy by updating this page.
